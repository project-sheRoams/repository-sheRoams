About the Project

SheRoams is the final project developed during the Bytes4Future Bootcamp, where we had two weeks to create an innovative digital solution. 
The goal of the project was to develop a platform dedicated exclusively to supporting women who want to explore the world independently, safely, and confidently.
The platform offers the tools and support necessary for users to travel safely and autonomously. 
Through the app, they have access to an engaged community of travelers, with up-to-date information on safety in different destinations, as well as personalized resources for travel planning. 
SheRoams aims to ensure that women can explore the world with confidence, with real-time support and relevant information, promoting inclusion and safety in the context of travel.
